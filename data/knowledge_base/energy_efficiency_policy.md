# Northstar Water Operations — Energy Efficiency Review Policy

> Demo policy only. Northstar Water Operations is fictional.

## Purpose

This policy defines when an increase in monthly energy consumption requires a formal equipment-efficiency investigation.

## Thresholds

1. **Energy increase of 20% or less:**
   - Review the change during routine monthly trend analysis.
   - A formal equipment-efficiency investigation is not mandatory solely because of the percentage increase.

2. **Energy increase greater than 20% and up to 30%:**
   - Initiate a formal equipment-efficiency investigation within five business days.
   - Validate energy-meter data and compare equipment runtime, throughput, maintenance history, and process conditions.

3. **Energy increase greater than 30%:**
   - Initiate the formal investigation immediately.
   - Notify the operations manager and energy program owner.

## Evidence standard

An energy increase by itself does not prove equipment inefficiency or failure. Analysts must distinguish reported facts from hypotheses and must not assign a cause unless supporting operational evidence exists.

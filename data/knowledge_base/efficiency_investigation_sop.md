# Northstar Water Operations — Equipment Efficiency Investigation SOP

> Demo SOP only. Northstar Water Operations is fictional.

## Objective

Provide a repeatable process for investigating abnormal energy consumption while avoiding unsupported conclusions.

## Investigation sequence

1. Validate the billing period and energy-meter readings.
2. Compare equipment runtime by major pump, blower, and treatment train.
3. Normalize energy use against treated flow where appropriate.
4. Review recent maintenance work orders and equipment-status alarms.
5. Check for abnormal starts, stops, cycling, or extended runtime.
6. Compare relevant process conditions and operating setpoints.
7. Document findings, evidence, unresolved questions, and recommended next actions.

## Decision rule

Do not label an equipment item as inefficient or failed based only on a month-over-month energy increase. A diagnosis requires corroborating evidence.

## Recommended output

The analyst's summary should contain:

- observed change,
- applicable policy threshold,
- evidence reviewed,
- confirmed findings,
- unknowns,
- recommended next step.
